FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Make math fun! Basic Math for Kids teaches addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division through colorful games and friendly challenges.

➕ FEATURES
• Four operations: +, −, ×, ÷
• Difficulty adjusts to your child's level
• Cute animations for correct answers
• Streak counter to build momentum
• Progress tracking per operation
• Star ratings to celebrate mastery
• Works fully offline

🎯 GAME MODES
• Practice — work through problems at your own pace
• Challenge — answer as many as possible in 60 seconds
• Quiz — test knowledge with multiple-choice

Designed for ages 5–10. Download free!
