A bunny hops, a block slides into the tray, and a child hears "3 plus 2 makes 5!" Basic Math for Kids is a calm first step into numbers for children aged 4 to 8, built around things a child can touch and move.

WHAT CHILDREN DO
- Count: tap each object to hear its number, then pick how many there are.
- Plus and minus: drag blocks into a tray or a bag, tap counters on a ten-frame, slide beads on a bead frame, or hop along a number line.
- Compare: which group has more, which number is bigger, and whether it is less than, the same as, or more than.
Five questions make a round, with no timers, no lives and no score to lose. If a child is stuck, Bea gently shows the answer step by step and counts along.

LEVELS THAT FOLLOW THE CHILD
Each skill adapts on its own. A few answers in a row and the numbers grow; a tricky patch and they step back. Free play covers numbers up to 10.

COLLECT STICKERS
Every round adds a sticker: 20 number cards and 12 friend stickers, with no coins and no shop for children.

FOR GROWN-UPS
This is early math only: counting, adding and taking away within 20, a number line and comparing. It does not cover times tables or counting past 20. Menus and spoken questions follow your language when your device has a voice for it; speech uses the device's own text-to-speech voice.
Settings, purchases and links sit behind a parental gate. There are no accounts and no chat, and we collect no personal data. Ads are a child-directed Google AdMob banner that never appears during play.
