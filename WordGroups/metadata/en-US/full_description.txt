The addictive word grouping game! Find four groups of four words that share a hidden connection. Sounds simple — it's deceptively tricky!

🔗 HOW TO PLAY
You're given 16 words. Sort them into 4 groups of 4 based on what they have in common. Each group is color-coded by difficulty: yellow (easy), green, blue, and purple (hardest).

✨ FEATURES
• Fresh puzzle every day
• 4 difficulty tiers per puzzle
• Unlimited attempts per day
• Mistake tracker (you get 4 lives)
• Reveal mode when stuck
• Weekly archives to replay
• Works fully offline
