Meeting Timer — Standup Clock

========================================
SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars max)
========================================
Time your standups and meetings. Each speaker gets a slot.

========================================
FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Run tighter, more efficient meetings! Meeting Timer lets you allocate time per agenda item or speaker and keeps everyone on track.

⏱️ FEATURES
• Set a total meeting time
• Add agenda items or speakers with time slots
• Tap to move to next item
• Visual countdown for each item
• Total time remaining display
• Warning alerts at 1 minute and 30 seconds
• Overtime indicator
• Works fully offline

✅ GREAT FOR
• Daily standups
• Sprint planning
• Presentations
• Panel discussions
• Any timed agenda

Download free and reclaim your meeting time!
