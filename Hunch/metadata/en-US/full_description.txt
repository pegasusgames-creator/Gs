The board knows a rule. You don't. Test. Observe. Crack it.

Hunch is a deduction game about thinking like a scientist. A hidden rule governs the 4x4 board — maybe it's about symmetry, maybe counts, maybe which cells touch. Build any pattern and hit TEST: green means it obeys, red means it breaks. Every experiment stays on the shelf, tinted by its verdict.

When your hunch hardens into a theory, classify three mystery boards. All three right and you've cracked it. The fewer tests you needed, the sharper the crack.

The rule catalog spans 19 rules across six families — counting, position, rows and columns, symmetry, comparison, connection — and the mystery boards are built so that only the true rule fits all three. A plausible-but-wrong theory always fails somewhere. That's the game.

FEATURES
