Bill Split makes it easy to divide restaurant bills, group meals, and shared costs — whether everyone pays equally or each person owes a different amount.

EQUAL SPLIT
Enter the bill, pick a tip percentage (10, 15, 18, 20, 25, or custom), and enter how many people are sharing. See the tip amount, total with tip, and per-person amount instantly.

CUSTOM SPLIT
Add people by name and type in what each person owes. Useful when different people ordered different things or one person is picking up part of the bill. Percentages update live.

HISTORY
Every saved split is stored in a history list so you can refer back to it or share the details later.

THEMES
Six colour themes: Forest (default), Ocean, Sunset, Violet, Gold, and Arctic. Unlock them with coins earned through use.

FEATURES
• Tip presets: 10%, 15%, 18%, 20%, 25%, custom
• Custom per-person amounts with live percentage display
• Split history with dates and details
• Coin rewards for regular use
• Works fully offline
