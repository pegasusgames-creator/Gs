Bill Split — Tip Calculator

========================================
SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars max)
========================================
Split bills & calculate tips easily between friends.

========================================
FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Never argue over the bill again! Bill Split makes it effortless to divide restaurant bills, calculate tips, and split costs fairly between any number of people.

🧾 FEATURES
• Enter total bill amount
• Choose tip percentage (10%, 15%, 18%, 20%, 25%, custom)
• Split between 1–20 people
• See per-person amount instantly
• Round up/down option
• Running total display
• Works fully offline

💡 ALSO INCLUDES
• Tip calculator standalone mode
• Percentage calculator
• Split unequal amounts by item

Fast, clean, and no fuss. Download free!
