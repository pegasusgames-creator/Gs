========================================
FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
A clean, reliable chess clock for over-the-board games. Supports all major time controls used in official and casual chess play.

♟️ FEATURES
• Tap your side to switch the clock
• Preset time controls: Bullet (1m), Blitz (3m, 5m), Rapid (10m, 15m, 30m), Classical (60m+)
• Fischer increment support
• Bronstein delay support
• Pause button for breaks
• Move counter for each player
• Loud warning sounds at low time
• Works fully offline

⏱️ TIME CONTROLS
• Bullet: 1 minute
• Blitz: 3+2, 5+0, 5+3
• Rapid: 10+0, 15+10, 30+0
• Classical: 60+30, 90+30

Also works for Go, Scrabble, backgammon, and any other timed board game. Download free!
