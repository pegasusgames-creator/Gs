A satisfying flood-fill puzzle. Choose a colour from the palette and watch it spread from the corner — your goal is to cover the entire grid in one colour within the move limit.

HOW TO PLAY
The grid fills from the upper-left corner. Tap a colour from the palette below to flood-fill the connected region. Keep choosing colours to expand your territory until the whole grid is the same colour.

100 LEVELS
Levels 1-30: 6x6 grid — quick and accessible.
Levels 31-70: 8x8 grid — more challenge.
Levels 71-100: 10x10 grid — demanding precision.
Each level has a unique puzzle with a specific move limit.

UNDO BUTTON
Changed your mind? Undo any move up to 10 times.

PERFECT CLEARS
Complete a level in under 60% of the move limit to earn a perfect clear — worth double coins.

6 COLOUR THEMES
Vivid, Warm, Cool, Pastel, Neon, Earth. Unlock themes with coins you earn from completed levels.

STATISTICS
Track levels cleared, perfect clears, coins earned, and your current win streak.

WORKS FULLY OFFLINE
No internet required. No account needed.
