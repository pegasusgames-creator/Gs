FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Your personal bartender! Cocktail Guide gives you 200+ classic and modern cocktail recipes with every ingredient, measurement, and mixing instruction you need.

🍹 FEATURES
• 200+ cocktail recipes
• Full ingredients list with measurements
• Step-by-step mixing instructions
• Filter by base spirit: Vodka, Gin, Rum, Tequila, Whiskey, etc.
• Filter by type: Sour, Fizz, Highball, Martini, Tropical
• Search by name or ingredient
• "I have these ingredients" finder
• Works fully offline

🍸 INCLUDES
Classic cocktails (Old Fashioned, Mojito, Negroni), modern favorites, mocktail alternatives, and party punches.

Drink responsibly. Download free!
