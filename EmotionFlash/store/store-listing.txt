========================================
FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Help children (and adults) identify and understand emotions with bright, friendly emotion flashcards!

💭 FEATURES
• 30+ emotion cards with facial expressions
• Emotion name and description on each card
• Flip mode: show face, guess the emotion
• Learn mode: browse all emotions with explanations
• Works fully offline

✅ GREAT FOR
• Teaching kids to name their feelings
• Social-emotional learning at home or school
• Speech therapy and ABA programs
• Adults building emotional vocabulary

Simple, gentle, and effective. Download free!
