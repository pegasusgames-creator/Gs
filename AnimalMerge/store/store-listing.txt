========================================
FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Tap and merge adorable animals to evolve them into bigger, rarer creatures! Animal Merge is a satisfying idle merge puzzle with dozens of animals to discover.

🐾 HOW TO PLAY
Drag two matching animals together to merge them into the next evolution. Keep merging to unlock bigger and wilder creatures. Fill your board wisely — space runs out fast!

🦁 FEATURES
• Dozens of animals across multiple evolution chains
• Smooth drag-and-merge mechanics
• Combo multipliers for chain merges
• Unlock rare and legendary animals
• Simple one-handed gameplay
• Satisfying merge animations
• Works fully offline

Can you reach the legendary tier? Download free and find out!
