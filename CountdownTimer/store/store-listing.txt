Count down to your most anticipated events! Whether it's a birthday, vacation, wedding, or holiday — Countdown Timer keeps the excitement building.

⏳ FEATURES
• Add unlimited countdown events
• Shows days, hours, minutes, seconds remaining
• Custom event names and emoji icons
• Set event date and time
• Widget support for home screen
• Notification reminders
• Past events shown as "X days ago"
• Works fully offline

Perfect for tracking birthdays, holidays, travel, weddings, graduations, and anything you're looking forward to!
