When you can't make up your mind, let the app decide! Decision Maker gives you multiple ways to make random choices — spinning wheel, yes/no, and custom option picker.

🎲 FEATURES
• Spin the wheel with your own custom options
• Yes / No random flip
• Pick a random number in any range
• Shake to decide
• Option list — add up to 20 choices, pick one randomly
• Works fully offline

✅ GREAT FOR
• Picking where to eat
• Choosing a movie
• Making fair group decisions
• Random name/team picker
• Any time you're stuck
