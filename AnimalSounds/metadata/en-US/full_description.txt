Tap any animal and hear its real sound! Animal Sounds is the perfect learning app for babies and toddlers — colorful, simple, and full of fun animal noises.

🐾 FEATURES
• 30+ animals with authentic sound effects
• Large, colorful animal buttons — easy for tiny fingers
• Animal names displayed clearly
• Learn mode: shows animal name + sound when tapped
• Quiz mode: "Which animal makes this sound?"
• Works fully offline — no wifi needed
• No timers, no pressure

🦁 ANIMALS INCLUDE
Farm animals (cow, pig, horse, chicken), wild animals (lion, elephant, tiger), ocean animals (whale, dolphin), birds, insects, and more!
