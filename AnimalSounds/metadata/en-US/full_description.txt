Tap a cow and it moos. Tap a lion and it roars. Animal Sounds is a calm sound board for children aged 3 to 8, with 44 original illustrated animals and a fox cub named Fenn who says hello and suggests who to meet next.

HEAR THE ANIMALS
Tap any animal on the sound board to hear its sound and its name. Open an animal's card for a big picture, where it lives and what it eats, then flip to the next one. The animal sounds are made by the app itself; they are friendly approximations, not recordings of real animals.

VISIT TEN HOMES
Farm, home, meadow, forest, jungle, savanna, ocean, polar lands, desert and riverbank: each page gathers the animals that live there. Tap an animal and Fenn tells you where it lives and what it eats. Every fact is checked.

PLAY GENTLE GAMES
- Who says this? Listen to a sound and tap the animal that makes it.
- Where does it live? Look at an animal and pick its home.
Five questions a round, with no timers, no lives and no score to lose. A wrong tap simply fades that picture and the sound plays again; after a couple of tries the right answer glows.

COLLECT STICKERS
Every right answer adds the animal's sticker, and every finished round adds a star sticker: 54 stickers to collect, with no coins and no shop for children.

MADE FOR SMALL HANDS
- Big buttons and pictures, so no reading is needed
- Fenn speaks the names and questions
- Lilac, Meadow and Bedtime color themes
- Works offline

FOR GROWN-UPS
Animal names and spoken questions follow your language (13 languages) when your device has a voice for it; speech uses the device's own text-to-speech voice. Sound words such as Moo stay English.
Settings, purchases and links sit behind a parental gate. There are no accounts and no chat, and we collect no personal data. Ads are a child-directed Google AdMob banner that never appears during the games.
Free to play: 22 animals in four homes (farm, home, meadow, forest) plus the Who says this? game. One optional purchase unlocks all 44 animals, all ten homes and the Where does it live? game and removes ads; a separate purchase removes ads only.
