# BreathingExercise — store paste sheet

One file with every translated string for every locale, wrapped in
`<locale>…</locale>` blocks. Open in Play Console / App Store Connect,
find the right field, and paste the matching block for each language.

The 13 locales (Play Console tags): en-US, ar, de-DE, es-419, fr-FR, hi-IN, id, it-IT, ja-JP, pt-BR, tr-TR, uk, zh-CN

**Notes:**
- Indonesian uses tag `<id>` (not `id-ID`) — Play Console quirk.
- Ukrainian uses tag `<uk>` (not `uk-UA`) — Play Console quirk.
- Chinese uses `<zh-CN>` (Simplified). Arabic uses `<ar>` (RTL — Play
  Console renders right-to-left automatically when this locale is
  enabled).
- Title is intentionally English globally (per TRANSLATIONS.md §3) —
  not included here. Leave the title field blank for non-English
  locales; Play falls back to the default.

<en-US>
Slow your breathing for a minute and let a glowing circle do the counting. Pick a rhythm, follow the circle as it grows and shrinks, and finish with a quiet note of how many minutes you have given yourself. Everything works offline and stays on your phone.

WHAT YOU GET
• A large, smooth inhale, hold and exhale animation (it stays still if you prefer reduced motion)
• Box 4-4-4-4, 4-7-8, coherent 5.5, the physiological sigh 2-1-6 and a relaxed 4-6 rhythm
• Optional soft tones, spoken cues in your language and gentle haptic ticks
• Sessions of 1, 2, 3 or 5 minutes that always finish on a full breath
• Streak, minutes logged, a 14-day chart and a calendar
• An optional gentle daily reminder, asked for only when you switch it on
• Backup and restore, light and dark themes, 13 languages

PLEASE NOTE
Breathing Exercise is for relaxation. It may help you relax, but it is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. If breathing exercises feel uncomfortable, stop and breathe normally. Talk to a health professional about any health concern.

PRIVACY
Your sessions stay on your device. No account, no sign-in.

PRO (one-time purchase, no subscription)
Custom patterns, sessions of 10 to 30 minutes, full history and per-pattern totals, extra accent colours and no ads. A separate one-time purchase removes ads only.
</en-US>
