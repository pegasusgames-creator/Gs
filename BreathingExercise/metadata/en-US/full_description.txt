Calm your mind in minutes with guided breathing exercises. Whether you need to reduce stress, improve focus, or fall asleep faster — this app has a breathing pattern for you.

🌬️ BREATHING PATTERNS
• Box Breathing (4-4-4-4) — focus and calm
• 4-7-8 Breathing — sleep and anxiety relief
• Belly Breathing — deep relaxation
• Energizing Breath — boost alertness
• Custom — set your own inhale/hold/exhale times

✨ FEATURES
• Visual breathing guide (expand/contract circle)
• Haptic feedback option
• Session timer (1–20 minutes)
• Background-friendly play
• Works fully offline
