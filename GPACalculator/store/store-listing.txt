FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Calculate your GPA in seconds! GPa Calculator supports weighted grades, semester tracking, and cumulative GPA calculation for high school and college students.

📊 FEATURES
• Semester-by-semester GPA calculation
• Cumulative GPA across all semesters
• Weighted GPA with credit hours
• Grade scale options (4.0, 5.0, letter grades)
• What-if calculator: "What grade do I need in this class?"
• Color-coded grade indicators
• Works fully offline

📚 SUPPORTS
• Standard 4.0 GPA scale
• Weighted AP/Honors courses (5.0 scale)
• Custom grade scales
• Pass/Fail courses

Download free and track your academic progress!
