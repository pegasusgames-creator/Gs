Create any QR code in seconds and scan codes with your camera! QR Code Generator covers everything from simple URLs to WiFi passwords and contact cards.

📷 GENERATE QR CODES FOR
• Website URLs
• Plain text
• WiFi network (SSID + password)
• Phone numbers
• Email addresses
• Contact cards (vCard)
• Location coordinates

✨ FEATURES
• Instant QR generation
• Custom colors and logo overlay (optional)
• Save QR codes to gallery
• Share QR codes directly
• Built-in scanner
• History of generated codes
• Works offline for generation (scanner needs camera)
