# FlappyBird — store paste sheet

One file with every translated string for every locale, wrapped in
`<locale>…</locale>` blocks. Open in Play Console / App Store Connect,
find the right field, and paste the matching block for each language.

The 13 locales (Play Console tags): en-US, ar, de-DE, es-419, fr-FR, hi-IN, id, it-IT, ja-JP, pt-BR, tr-TR, uk, zh-CN

**Notes:**
- Indonesian uses tag `<id>` (not `id-ID`) — Play Console quirk.
- Ukrainian uses tag `<uk>` (not `uk-UA`) — Play Console quirk.
- Chinese uses `<zh-CN>` (Simplified). Arabic uses `<ar>` (RTL — Play
  Console renders right-to-left automatically when this locale is
  enabled).
- Title is intentionally English globally (per TRANSLATIONS.md §3) —
  not included here. Leave the title field blank for non-English
  locales; Play falls back to the default.

<en-US>
The addictive one-tap flying game. Tap to keep your bird in the air and fly through gaps between pipes. Simple to learn, impossible to put down.

HOW TO PLAY
Tap the screen to flap upward. Time your taps to navigate through pipe gaps. Each pipe you clear scores a point. Don't touch the pipes or the ground!

6 BIRD CHARACTERS
Start with the classic Yellow bird. Unlock Blue, Red, Green, Pink, and the dazzling Rainbow bird using coins you earn while playing.

MEDAL SYSTEM
Earn medals based on your high score: Starter, Bronze at 10, Silver at 20, Gold at 40. Chase each tier to prove your skills.

SCORE HISTORY
Your last 10 runs are saved automatically. Track your progress and see how your personal record grew over time.

DAY & NIGHT CYCLE
The sky gradually shifts from dawn to dusk as you play. Watch the background change colour with every run.

PARTICLE EFFECTS
Every pipe you clear triggers a burst of colour particles. Screen shake on death adds a satisfying punch to every collision.

EARN COINS WHILE YOU FLY
Coins appear in pipe gaps — fly through them to collect. Use coins to unlock new bird characters in the shop.

WORKS FULLY OFFLINE
No internet required. No account needed. Pure arcade flying.
</en-US>
