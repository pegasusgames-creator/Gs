Flappy Wings — Tap to Fly

========================================
SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars max)
========================================
Tap to flap through the pipes! Classic arcade challenge.

========================================
FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
The addictive one-tap flying game! Tap to keep your bird in the air and fly through gaps between pipes. Simple rules, endless challenge!

🐦 HOW TO PLAY
Tap the screen to make the bird flap upward. Time your taps to navigate through the pipe gaps. Every gap you pass through scores one point. Don't touch the pipes!

✨ FEATURES
• Classic one-tap gameplay
• Smooth physics and animations
• High score tracking
• Multiple bird skins to unlock
• Medal system: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum
• Works fully offline

Easy to learn — brutally hard to master. Download free!
