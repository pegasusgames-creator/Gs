Drop fruits into the jar and merge matching ones to create bigger, rarer fruits! A satisfying physics-based merge puzzle inspired by Suika Game.

🍎 HOW TO PLAY
Drop fruits from the top. When two same fruits touch, they merge into the next bigger fruit. Combine all the way up to the legendary Watermelon! Don't let the pile overflow!

✨ FEATURES
• 11 fruit tiers from cherry to watermelon
• Realistic drop physics
• Combo scoring for chain merges
• High score tracking
• Next fruit preview
• Works fully offline
