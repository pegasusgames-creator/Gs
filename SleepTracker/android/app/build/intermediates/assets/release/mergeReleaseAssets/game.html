Tips for Better Sleep
Keep a consistent sleep schedule, even on weekends.
Avoid screens 1 hour before bedtime. Use night mode.
Keep the bedroom cool (65-68F / 18-20C) and dark.
Avoid caffeine after 2 PM. Alcohol disrupts deep sleep.
Exercise regularly, but not within 3 hours of sleep.
Try 4-7-8 breathing or progressive muscle relaxation.
