FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Test your knowledge of world flags! Flag Quiz shows you a flag and challenges you to identify the country — from easy classics to obscure territories.

🏳️ FEATURES
• 200+ country flags
• Multiple choice and type-the-answer modes
• Continent filters: Africa, Asia, Europe, Americas, Oceania
• Flashcard study mode before quizzing
• Difficulty levels: Easy (major nations), Hard (all territories)
• Streak tracker
• Flag fact revealed after each answer
• Works fully offline

From the Union Jack to the flag of Bhutan — can you identify them all? Download free!
