<en-US>
Two tiles. Same picture. Both free. Tap them and they lift away — that is the whole rule, and it never stops being satisfying.

Mahjong Solitaire is the classic tile-matching game made easy to read and relaxing to play. The tiles are large and crisp, free tiles stay bright while held ones dim, and nothing rushes you.

WHY IT FEELS DIFFERENT
• Every deal can be cleared. Each board is dealt in reverse from a finished game, so a full solution always exists, and all 500 levels are checked before release.
• Big, clear tiles. Our own tile artwork in strong colours, sized for arm's-length play on phones and tablets.
• High-contrast tiles. One switch in Settings gives whiter faces, darker inks and corner numbers.
• Free undo, always. Take back as many pairs as you like.
• Hints that help. A hint lights a pair that keeps the board clearable from where you are, not just any match.
• Shuffle without dead ends. Stuck? Shuffle re-deals the tiles left on the board so they can all still be cleared.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 500 levels that grow from a 14-tile warm-up to boards of 120+ tiles
• 21 layout families: the traditional Turtle plus Pyramid, Lantern, Lotus, Moon Gate, Pagoda, Butterfly, Spiral, Fortress, Heart, Garden Wall and more
• A new daily board every day, small on Monday and grand on Sunday, with a streak to keep
• Endless mode with fresh boards whenever you want more
• Stars for clearing under par time and without a shuffle
• Seven table themes, from Honey Teak to Midnight
• Gentle sounds and optional vibration
• Plays offline: no internet needed

HOW TO PLAY
Match two identical tiles to remove them. A tile is free when no other tile rests on it and its left or right side is open. Flowers match any flower and seasons match any season. Clear every tile to finish the board.

Mahjong Solitaire is free to play, with optional ads and in-app purchases (coins, hints, ad removal and an optional Season Pass). Questions or ideas? Write to pegasusgames@atomicmail.io
</en-US>
