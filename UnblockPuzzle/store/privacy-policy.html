1. Introduction

Welcome to Unblock Puzzle, developed and published by Pegasus Games ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of the Unblock Puzzle mobile application (the "App"), available on Google Play under the package identifier com.pegasusgames.unblock.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Please read this policy carefully. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

This policy applies solely to information processed in connection with the App. It does not apply to third-party websites or services that may be linked from within the App.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying details in order to play the game.

Key point: All gameplay progress — including completed levels, coin balance, hint usage, daily streak, and settings — is stored exclusively on your device using the device's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

The following types of data may be present on your device locally:

  • Gameplay progress — which levels you have completed, your current level, and star ratings.
  • Virtual currency balance — the number of in-game coins you have earned or purchased.
  • Hint and lives state — how many hints remain and your lives regeneration timer.
  • Daily challenge streak — consecutive days you have completed the daily puzzle.
  • App preferences — sound and music on/off settings.

None of this data is transmitted to our servers or any third party by us directly. It exists solely to allow the App to remember your progress between sessions.

Automatically Collected Technical Data

Certain third-party SDKs integrated into the App (see Section 3) may automatically collect limited technical information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, and crash reports as part of their own operation. We do not control this data collection; it is governed by the respective third party's privacy policy.

Google AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (e.g., Android Advertising ID) to show you interest-based advertisements. You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (or "Delete advertising ID" on Android 12+).

Purchasing the "Remove Ads" in-app product will disable all advertisement display within the App.

In-App Purchases

All payment processing for in-app purchases is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We only receive a confirmation token from Google Play indicating whether a purchase was successful.

4. Children's Privacy

Unblock Puzzle is a general-audience puzzle game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

Because we do not collect any personal data ourselves, the App does not pose inherent data-collection risks for children. However, third-party advertising (Google AdMob) is present by default. Parents or guardians who wish to ensure no interest-based advertising is shown can either:

  • Reset or disable the Android Advertising ID in device settings, or
  • Purchase the "Remove Ads" in-app product to disable all ads entirely.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, please contact us immediately at support@pegasusgames.com and we will take prompt steps to address the situation.
