FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Take control of your finances! Budget Planner helps you set a monthly budget, track every expense, and see where your money is going with clear charts.

💰 FEATURES
• Set monthly income and budget
• Add expenses by category (Food, Transport, Bills, etc.)
• Spending chart shows category breakdown
• Budget vs actual comparison
• Running balance display
• Weekly and monthly summaries
• All data stored privately on device
• Works fully offline

💡 CATEGORIES INCLUDED
Food, Transport, Housing, Healthcare, Entertainment, Shopping, Savings, and Custom.
