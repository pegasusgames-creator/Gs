FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Cook the perfect egg every time! Egg Timer gives you precise countdown timers for soft, medium, and hard boiled eggs — plus a fully customizable timer for any food.

🥚 EGG PRESETS
• Soft boiled: 3 minutes (runny yolk)
• Medium boiled: 6 minutes (jammy yolk)
• Hard boiled: 10 minutes (fully set)
• Extra hard: 15 minutes (firm throughout)

✨ FEATURES
• Visual progress timer with egg animation
• Loud alert when done
• Vibration option
• Custom timer for any duration (1–60 minutes)
• Pause and resume
• Works fully offline

Never overcook your eggs again! Download free!
