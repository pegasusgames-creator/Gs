The kitchen timer that keeps up with your cooking. Run multiple timers at once, pick from built-in presets, and get a clear audio alert when anything is done.

RUN MULTIPLE TIMERS
Add as many timers as you need — eggs, pasta, and vegetables all timing simultaneously. Each timer has its own progress ring and countdown display.

18 COOKING PRESETS
One tap to start a preset:
Eggs: Soft (3 min), Medium (5 min), Hard (10 min), Poached, Fried, Scrambled
Pasta: Spaghetti, Penne, Ramen, Noodles, Rice, Quinoa
Vegetables: Broccoli, Carrots, Corn, Peas, Potatoes, Asparagus

CUSTOM TIMERS
Set any time from 1 second to 99 minutes and give it a name. Your custom timer works just like any preset.

AUDIO ALERTS
Three-tone beep sequence when a timer finishes — clear enough to hear from across the kitchen.

6 COLOUR THEMES
Amber, Ocean, Forest, Rose, Night, Cream. Unlock themes with coins you earn each time a timer completes.

LIFETIME STATISTICS
Track total timers completed, coins earned, total time tracked, and today's session count.

WORKS FULLY OFFLINE
No internet required. No account needed. Just set a timer and cook.
