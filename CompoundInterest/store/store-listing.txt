FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Watch your savings grow! The Compound Interest Calculator shows you exactly how much your investment will be worth over time with different interest rates and compounding frequencies.

📈 FEATURES
• Principal, rate, time, and compounding frequency inputs
• Compounding options: daily, monthly, quarterly, yearly
• Growth chart showing value over time
• Breakdown of principal vs interest earned
• Compare two scenarios side by side
• Works fully offline

💡 GREAT FOR
• Comparing savings accounts
• Planning retirement contributions
• Understanding investment growth
• Student finance education

Download free and start planning your financial future!
