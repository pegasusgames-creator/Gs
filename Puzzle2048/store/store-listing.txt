APP NAME
2048 Puzzle

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars max)

FULL DESCRIPTION
2048 Puzzle is the ultimate number merging game that's easy to learn but impossible to put down!

HOW TO PLAY:
Swipe up, down, left, or right to slide all tiles on the board. When two tiles with the same number collide, they merge into one! Reach the legendary 2048 tile — and beyond!
