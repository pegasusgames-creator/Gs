Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2024

Pegasus Games ("we", "our", or "us") operates the 2048 Puzzle mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect or store any personally identifiable information. However, third-party services integrated into the App may collect information as described below.

2. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (Advertising)

We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, and usage data to deliver targeted advertising. You can opt out of personalized ads via your device's advertising settings.

  • AdMob Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment information. All billing is handled by Google.

  • Google Play Terms: https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/

3. Local Data Storage

Game progress, scores, and settings are saved locally on your device using browser localStorage (within our WebView). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

4. Permissions

The App requests the following permissions:

  • INTERNET — Required to load advertisements and font resources.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Used to check network availability before loading ads.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING — Required for in-app purchases.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App is rated "Everyone" and does not target children specifically. AdMob is configured to comply with COPPA regulations. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

6. Data Retention

We do not retain user data on our servers. Game data stored locally on your device is retained until you uninstall the App or manually reset your progress within the App settings.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect the security of the App. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • Right to access your data
  • Right to delete your data
  • Right to opt out of personalized advertising

To exercise these rights regarding AdMob data, please use your device's advertising settings or contact Google directly.
