========================================
FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
A fun and fast emoji sorting game! Drag emojis into the correct category buckets before the timer runs out. Speed and accuracy win!

😊 HOW TO PLAY
Emojis appear at the top. Drag each one into the matching category bucket (Animals, Food, Sports, Faces, etc.). The faster and more accurate you are, the higher you score!

✨ FEATURES
• Multiple emoji categories
• Progressive difficulty — more emojis, faster pace
• Combo multiplier for correct streaks
• Daily challenge mode
• High score tracking
• Works fully offline

Great for kids and adults! Download free!
