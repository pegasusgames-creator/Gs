========================================
FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Slide the colored blocks off the board in the right order! Color Block Jam is a satisfying puzzle game where you need to clear all blocks without getting stuck.

🟥 HOW TO PLAY
Tap a block to select it, then swipe to slide it in the available direction. Blocks can only move in one axis. Clear all blocks to complete the level!

🧩 FEATURES
• 200+ levels with increasing challenge
• Multiple block sizes and orientations
• No time pressure — think at your own pace
• Undo button for mistakes
• Clean, colorful visual design
• Works fully offline

Fans of UnBlock Puzzle and Rush Hour will love this. Download free!
