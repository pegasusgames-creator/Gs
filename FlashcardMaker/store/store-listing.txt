Make your own flashcards for any subject and study smarter! Flashcard Maker lets you create, organize, and review card decks with spaced repetition.

🗒️ FEATURES
• Create unlimited card decks
• Add question/answer or front/back format
• Spaced repetition algorithm for efficient review
• Swipe left (wrong) / right (correct) interface
• Text formatting (bold, italic)
• Shuffle deck option
• Progress tracking per deck
• Works fully offline
