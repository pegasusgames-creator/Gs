The most satisfying stress relief app on Android. Pop bubble wrap any time you need to decompress.

JUST POP
Tap individual bubbles or drag your finger to pop a whole row in one swipe. Each bubble plays a crisp pop sound and a satisfying press animation.

THREE BUBBLE SIZES
Switch between Small (10×14 grid, 140 bubbles), Medium (10×14), and Large (7×10, 70 bubbles). Choose your preferred bubble size for maximum satisfaction.

POP ROW & POP ALL
Too stressed for one at a time? Tap Pop Row to burst a full row in one go, or Pop All to clear the entire sheet with a cascading pop wave.

6 COLOUR THEMES
Classic Blue, Pastel Purple, Mint Green, Coral, Gold, and Neon. Unlock themes with coins you earn by popping.

LIFETIME STATISTICS
Track total lifetime pops, sessions, full sheets completed, and your current pop streak.

EARN COINS WHILE YOU POP
Earn coins every 20 pops, for every streak of 10 in a row, and every time you complete a full sheet. Use coins to unlock colour themes.

WORKS FULLY OFFLINE
No internet required. No account needed. Pure, offline bubble wrap.
