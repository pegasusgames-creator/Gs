========================================
FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
A beautifully animated coin flip for any decision! Flip heads or tails with a realistic 3D coin animation, track your results, and see your flip history.

🪙 FEATURES
• Realistic 3D coin flip animation
• Heads or Tails result every time
• Running tally of heads and tails count
• Streak counter (current and best)
• Flip history (last 20 results)
• Shake phone to flip (optional)
• True random result every time
• Works fully offline

Perfect for making quick decisions, settling arguments, or just satisfying curiosity. Download free!
