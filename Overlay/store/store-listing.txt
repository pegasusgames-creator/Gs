APP NAME: Pipe Connect

SHORT DESC: Connect the colored pipes and fill the grid! Relaxing logic puzzle.

FULL DESC:
Connect matching colored endpoints with pipes. Fill every cell to solve the level. 150 levels from 5×5 to 10×10, daily challenge, offline play, no timers.

FEATURES:
• 150 hand-crafted levels — 5×5 beginner to 10×10 master
• Simple drag-to-draw controls — touch or mouse
• Every cell must be filled to complete a level
• Daily Challenge with streak rewards
• 3-star rating — can you solve every level without resetting?
• Earn coins and hints
• Fully offline — no internet required
• No timers, no pressure — play at your own pace
• Satisfying completion sounds and animations

HOW TO PLAY:
1. Drag from one colored dot to its matching dot
2. Draw pipes to connect all pairs
3. Fill every cell on the grid
4. Pipes cannot cross — plan your path!

KEYWORDS: pipe puzzle, flow free, connect pipes, color connect, brain puzzle, relaxing, offline
