Fishing Log — Catch Tracker

========================================
SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars max)
========================================
Log your fishing catches with species, weight & location.

========================================
FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Track every fish you catch! Fishing Log helps anglers record catches, note locations, log weather conditions, and track their personal records.

🎣 FEATURES
• Log species, weight, length, and date
• Add fishing location (GPS or manual)
• Weather conditions at catch time
• Photo attachment for each catch
• Personal best tracker per species
• Total catch statistics
• Export log as text
• Works fully offline

📊 STATS TRACKED
• Total catches by species
• Personal records
• Best fishing spots
• Seasonal patterns

Perfect for recreational and competitive anglers. Download free!
