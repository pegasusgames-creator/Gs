FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Track every expense in seconds! Expense Tracker helps you log daily spending, categorize it, and see clear charts of where your money is going.

💳 FEATURES
• Quick-add expense with amount, category, and note
• 15 spending categories (Food, Transport, Bills, etc.)
• Daily, weekly, monthly summaries
• Pie chart breakdown by category
• Running monthly total
• Export expenses as text
• All data stored locally on device
• Works fully offline

💡 CATEGORIES
Food & Dining, Transport, Shopping, Entertainment, Bills & Utilities, Health, Travel, Personal Care, Education, Gifts, and more.
