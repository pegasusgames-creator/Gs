The famous Stroop Effect test — words appear in a different ink color than their meaning. Tap the color of the INK, not what the word says. Sounds simple until your brain fights itself.

HOW TO PLAY
A color word appears on screen — for example RED written in blue ink. Tap the BLUE button, not red. Your brain wants to read the word; you must override it and focus on the ink color instead.

GAME MODES
30 seconds — quick warm-up
60 seconds — full challenge
100 Questions — accuracy marathon (no time pressure)

FEATURES
• Four answer buttons: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow
• Millisecond reaction time shown after each answer
• Score increases for correct answers; deducted 0.5 for wrong
• Session history with score, accuracy, avg RT, and rating
• All-time record and cumulative stats
• 6 colour themes for the play screen

RATINGS
Brilliant (95%+ acc, sub 400ms), Excellent (90%+), Good (80%+), Keep Training

Works fully offline.
