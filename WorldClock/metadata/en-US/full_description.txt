World Clock shows live times across 42 major cities, organized by region. Build your personal clock list and see at a glance who is awake, working, or asleep — no more mental math.

FEATURES
• 42 cities across 6 continents — Americas, Europe, Africa, Middle East, Asia, Oceania
• Live daytime/nighttime indicator on each clock
• UTC offset shown on every card
• Add and remove cities with a single tap
• 6 color themes to match your style (Ocean, Sunset, Forest, Neon, Gold, Ice)
• Coin economy — earn coins by using the app and watching optional ads
• Statistics screen tracks opens, sessions, and coins earned
