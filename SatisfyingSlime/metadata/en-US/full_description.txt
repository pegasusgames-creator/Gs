Satisfying Slime is a relaxing physics toy — poke, drag and swirl a blob of virtual slime that squishes and stretches under your finger. Each touch pulls the particles toward your fingertip and they slowly reform when you lift.

COLORS
Ten slime colors to unlock: Aqua, Lavender, Mint, Peach, Pink, Gold, Cobalt, Coral, Silver, and Rainbow (color-cycling). The first three are free; the rest unlock with coins earned through play.

EFFECTS
Four physics modes change how the slime moves:
Normal — smooth and cohesive
Glitter — white sparkle particles trail your touch
Bubble — particles float upward with a translucent bubble look
Gooey — thick and slow, particles cling together longer

EARN COINS
A coin drops every 50 touches. Watch an optional rewarded ad for 10 coins instantly. Use coins to unlock colors and effects.

Works fully offline.
