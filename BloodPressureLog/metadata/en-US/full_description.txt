PLEASE NOTE
Blood Pressure Log is a diary, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and a category is not a diagnosis. Home readings vary with the device, cuff size and the moment. Talk to a doctor or other health professional about your readings and any treatment. In an emergency call your local emergency number.

CATEGORIES (2017 ACC/AHA, adults)
Normal: below 120 and below 80 · Elevated: 120–129 and below 80 · Stage 1: 130–139 or 80–89 · Stage 2: 140 or higher or 90 or higher · Hypertensive crisis: above 180 and/or above 120.
