# BloodPressureLog — store paste sheet

One file with every translated string for every locale, wrapped in
`<locale>…</locale>` blocks. Open in Play Console / App Store Connect,
find the right field, and paste the matching block for each language.

The 13 locales (Play Console tags): en-US, ar, de-DE, es-419, fr-FR, hi-IN, id, it-IT, ja-JP, pt-BR, tr-TR, uk, zh-CN

**Notes:**
- Indonesian uses tag `<id>` (not `id-ID`) — Play Console quirk.
- Ukrainian uses tag `<uk>` (not `uk-UA`) — Play Console quirk.
- Chinese uses `<zh-CN>` (Simplified). Arabic uses `<ar>` (RTL — Play
  Console renders right-to-left automatically when this locale is
  enabled).
- Title is intentionally English globally (per TRANSLATIONS.md §3) —
  not included here. Leave the title field blank for non-English
  locales; Play falls back to the default.

<en-US>
Save a blood pressure reading in seconds and see which category of the 2017 ACC/AHA guideline it falls in. Follow your averages and trends over 7, 30 and 90 days and share a plain summary with your doctor. Everything stays on your phone, works offline and needs no account.

WHAT YOU GET
• Log systolic, diastolic and pulse with date and time, arm and position, tags (coffee, after exercise, stress, medication…) and a note
• Colour and name for every reading, in neutral wording such as "this reading falls in the Stage 1 category of the 2017 ACC/AHA guideline"
• Charts for 7, 30 and 90 days, averages, pulse average and morning versus evening comparison
• A calm "seek medical help" note for readings of 180/120 or higher
• Doctor summary you can share as text, backup and restore, light and dark themes, 13 languages
• Optional reminders: morning and evening measurement, medication time, a re-check at a chosen date and time, an appointment reminder

PLEASE NOTE
Blood Pressure Log is a diary, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and a category is not a diagnosis. Home readings vary with the device, cuff size and the moment. Talk to a doctor or other health professional about your readings and any treatment. In an emergency call your local emergency number.

CATEGORIES (2017 ACC/AHA, adults)
Normal: below 120 and below 80 · Elevated: 120–129 and below 80 · Stage 1: 130–139 or 80–89 · Stage 2: 140 or higher or 90 or higher · Hypertensive crisis: above 180 and/or above 120.

PRIVACY
Your readings stay on your device. No account, no sign-in.

PRO (one-time purchase, no subscription)
Unlimited history (free: 60 readings), CSV export, extra accent colours and no ads. A separate one-time purchase removes ads only.
</en-US>
