Blood Pressure Log & Tracker

========================================
SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars max)
========================================
Track your blood pressure readings with charts & history.

========================================
FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Keep a simple, clean log of your blood pressure readings. Track trends, spot patterns, and share data with your doctor — all stored privately on your device.

💓 FEATURES
• Log systolic, diastolic, and pulse readings
• Timestamp every entry automatically
• Color-coded status: Normal, Elevated, High Stage 1/2
• Visual chart showing 7-day, 30-day, 90-day trends
• Export readings as text for your doctor
• Optional notes per reading
• All data stored locally — never uploaded
• Works fully offline

📊 BLOOD PRESSURE RANGES SHOWN
• Normal: < 120/80
• Elevated: 120–129/< 80
• High Stage 1: 130–139/80–89
• High Stage 2: ≥ 140/90

Not a medical device. Always consult your doctor. Download free!
