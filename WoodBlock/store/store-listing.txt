FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
The relaxing wood block puzzle game! Drag and drop wooden pieces onto the grid to complete lines and score points. No timer, no pressure — just pure focus.

🪵 HOW TO PLAY
Drag block shapes from the bottom onto the 9×9 board. Complete full lines (horizontal or vertical) to clear them and score. Game over when no pieces can be placed.

✨ FEATURES
• Classic 9×9 block puzzle
• Wide variety of block shapes
• Line clear combo scoring
• No time pressure
• High score tracking
• Warm wood aesthetic
• Works fully offline
