Keep your household running smoothly! Chore Chart helps families assign tasks, track who did what, and celebrate completed chores.

🧹 FEATURES
• Add family members with custom names and colors
• Create chore tasks and assign to members
• Mark chores as done with a tap
• Daily, weekly, or custom repeat schedules
• Completion streaks to encourage consistency
• Emoji icons for each chore
• All data stored locally
• Works fully offline

🏡 GREAT FOR
• Parents managing kids' chores
• Roommates splitting house tasks
• Personal task tracking
• Weekly cleaning routines
