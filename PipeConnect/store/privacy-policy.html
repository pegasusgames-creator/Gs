Privacy Policy

App: Pipe Connect  |  Package: com.pegasusgames.pipeconnect
Last updated: March 19, 2026

Pegasus Games ("we", "us", "our") built Pipe Connect as a free-to-play mobile game supported by optional in-app purchases and advertising. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or location. All game progress (levels completed, coins, settings) is stored locally on your device using the browser's localStorage and Android's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Automatically Collected Information

We do not operate any analytics or tracking infrastructure of our own. However, third-party services integrated into the app (described below) may collect certain non-personal, aggregated data as described in their own privacy policies.

2. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (Advertising)

Pipe Connect uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve relevant ads. This data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting https://www.google.com/settings/ads or through your device's advertising settings.

If you purchase the "Remove Ads" product, no ad SDK calls are made during gameplay.

Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases)

Pipe Connect offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. All payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive, store, or process your payment card details. Purchase history is managed by Google Play and subject to Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

  • Google Play Terms of Service: https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/

3. Children's Privacy

Pipe Connect is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data through our app, please contact us at the address below and we will take steps to remove such information.

4. Data Retention

All game data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all saved data at any time from within the app (Settings → Reset All Data) or by uninstalling the app. We have no access to or copies of your local game data.

5. Security

Since we do not collect or transmit your personal data to any server we control, there is no database or server-side storage to secure on our end. Your local game data is protected by your device's own operating system security.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
