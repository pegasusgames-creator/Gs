Somewhere in this grid, ten ocean words are hiding. Run your finger across one and it lights up like a highlighter on paper.

Word Search is the classic puzzle-book game, rebuilt to be calm and easy on the eyes. Big, crisp letters, a highlight that snaps neatly into line, and no pressure unless you want to beat the clock.

WHY IT FEELS GOOD TO PLAY
• Big, readable letters on a warm paper board, sized for phones and tablets.
• Swipe in any of the 8 directions: the highlight snaps straight across, down or diagonally. Prefer tapping? Tap the first letter, then the last.
• Every listed word appears exactly once in its grid. All 500 grids are checked before release, and daily and endless grids are checked the same way on your device.
• Family-friendly word lists, picked by hand. Every grid is screened in all 8 directions against a list of rude words.
• Hints that help: a hint flashes the first letter of a word you still need.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 500 themed puzzles that grow from small 6×6 warm-ups to 15×15 grids, with 8 to 20 words each
• 60 themes, including Ocean, Space, Fruits, Birds, Dinosaurs, Desserts, Instruments, Pirates, Gems and Rainforest
• Directions open up gently: across and down first, then diagonals, then backwards, then all eight ways
• Hidden bonus words on many grids: spot the extra word that is not on the list to earn coins
• Hidden-message endings: on some levels the leftover letters spell a secret line or a fun fact
• A new daily themed grid every day, small on Monday and big on Sunday, with a streak to keep
• Endless mode with fresh grids whenever you want more
• Stars for beating the par time and for solving without hints
• Seven board themes, from Heather and Newsprint to Sea Glass and Midnight
• Gentle synthesized sounds and optional vibration
• Works fully offline

A NOTE ON LANGUAGE
The puzzles and word lists are in English.

FAIR AND FRIENDLY
No account needed. Free to play with optional ads and in-app purchases: coin packs, hint packs, Remove Ads and an optional Season or Weekly Pass. Campaign levels and the daily grid never cost a life; lives are only used for Endless grids.
