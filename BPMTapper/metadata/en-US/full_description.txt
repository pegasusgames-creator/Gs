Tap along to any beat to instantly measure its BPM (beats per minute). Perfect for musicians, DJs, fitness enthusiasts, and anyone who needs to know a song's tempo.

🎵 HOW TO USE
Simply tap the button in time with the beat. The BPM is calculated from your taps in real time and stabilizes after a few taps.

✨ FEATURES
• Real-time BPM calculation from taps
• Running average for accuracy
• Tempo labels: Largo, Andante, Allegro, Presto, etc.
• Reset button to start over
• Large tap button — easy to hit in time
• Works fully offline
