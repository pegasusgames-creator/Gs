FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Boost your productivity with the proven Pomodoro Technique! Work for 25 minutes, take a 5-minute break, repeat. After 4 rounds, take a longer break.

🍅 HOW IT WORKS
• 25-minute work session
• 5-minute short break
• 15-minute long break (every 4 pomodoros)
• Repeat and stay in the zone!

✨ FEATURES
• Customizable work/break durations
• Completed pomodoro counter
• Daily session goal setting
• Notification sound when timer ends
• Session history log
• Works fully offline

Download free and stop procrastinating!
