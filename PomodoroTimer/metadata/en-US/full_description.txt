A clean, focused Pomodoro timer for deep work. Work in focused sprints, take structured breaks, and build a daily focus habit with streak tracking.

HOW IT WORKS
Work for a set time (default 25 minutes), then take a short break (5 minutes). After completing a full cycle of focus sessions, reward yourself with a long break (15 minutes). This rhythm is the Pomodoro Technique.

FULLY CUSTOMIZABLE
Adjust focus duration (5–60 min), short break (1–30 min), and long break (5–60 min) to match your workflow. Set the number of sessions per cycle from 2 to 8.

AUTO-START OPTION
Enable auto-start and the next session begins automatically after each transition — no interruptions to your flow.

TASK LABEL
Name each focus session to stay clear on what you're working on. Handy reminder visible while the timer runs.

6 FOCUS THEMES
Tomato, Ocean, Forest, Night, Sunset, Mono. Unlock themes with coins earned for completing focus sessions.

STATISTICS
Track total pomodoros completed, total focus time, today's sessions, and your daily focus streak.

WORKS FULLY OFFLINE
No internet required. No account needed.
