# BloodSugarLog — store paste sheet

One file with every translated string for every locale, wrapped in
`<locale>…</locale>` blocks. Open in Play Console / App Store Connect,
find the right field, and paste the matching block for each language.

The 13 locales (Play Console tags): en-US, ar, de-DE, es-419, fr-FR, hi-IN, id, it-IT, ja-JP, pt-BR, tr-TR, uk, zh-CN

**Notes:**
- Indonesian uses tag `<id>` (not `id-ID`) — Play Console quirk.
- Ukrainian uses tag `<uk>` (not `uk-UA`) — Play Console quirk.
- Chinese uses `<zh-CN>` (Simplified). Arabic uses `<ar>` (RTL — Play
  Console renders right-to-left automatically when this locale is
  enabled).
- Title is intentionally English globally (per TRANSLATIONS.md §3) —
  not included here. Leave the title field blank for non-English
  locales; Play falls back to the default.

<en-US>
Log a blood sugar reading in seconds, in mg/dL or mmol/L, and see it coloured against the target range YOU set. Charts, time in range and averages show your patterns. Everything stays on your phone: no account, and it works offline.

WHAT YOU GET
• Fast log: type the number, pick Fasting, Before meal, After meal, Bedtime or Other, add carbs, tags and a note
• Exact unit conversion: 1 mmol/L = 18.0156 mg/dL, stored as whole mg/dL and shown to one decimal in mmol/L
• Your ranges: a neutral default you replace with the ranges your clinician gave you, optionally per meal context
• Insights: time in range (share of readings), readings over time, a day-pattern chart, averages by time of day and by meal
• Estimated A1C, shown only with enough data (20 readings on 10 days in the last 90) and labelled as an estimate, with the ADAG formula: (average mg/dL + 46.7) ÷ 28.7
• Doctor summary: a plain-text summary to read out or share
• Testing reminders, weekly or on a set date, only if they fit your plan
• Backup and restore, light and dark themes, 13 languages

PLEASE NOTE
This is a logbook, not a medical device. It does not diagnose and gives no dosing or insulin advice; there is no calculator for either. Colours follow the ranges you enter. The A1C figure is an estimate from spot checks, not a lab result. If you feel unwell or are unsure what to do, contact your care team or your local emergency number.

PRIVACY
Your readings stay on your device. No account, no sign-in.

PRO (one-time purchase, no subscription)
Unlimited history (free: 60 readings), CSV export, extra accent colours and no ads. A separate one-time purchase removes ads only.
</en-US>
