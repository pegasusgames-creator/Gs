Blood Sugar Log & Tracker

========================================
SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars max)
========================================
Log blood glucose readings with charts and meal notes.

========================================
FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Simple blood sugar tracking for diabetics and health-conscious users. Log your glucose readings, add meal notes, and visualize your trends.

🩸 FEATURES
• Log glucose in mg/dL or mmol/L
• Pre/post-meal tags for each reading
• Color-coded ranges (Low, Normal, High)
• 7-day and 30-day trend charts
• Optional notes with each entry
• A1C estimate based on recent averages
• All data stored locally on device
• Works fully offline

📊 REFERENCE RANGES SHOWN
• Fasting normal: 70–99 mg/dL
• Post-meal normal: < 140 mg/dL
• Pre-diabetic range highlighted

Not a medical device. Consult your healthcare provider. Download free!
