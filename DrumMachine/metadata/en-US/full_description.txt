Make your own beats! Drum Machine is a fun, easy-to-use 8-step sequencer with real drum samples.

🥁 FEATURES
• 8-step sequencer for beat programming
• 8 drum pads: kick, snare, hi-hat (open/closed), clap, rim, cowbell, cymbal
• Adjustable BPM (40–200)
• Loop playback
• Multiple drum kit presets: Rock, Electronic, Hip-Hop, Latin
• Live pad mode for freestyle tapping
• Works fully offline
