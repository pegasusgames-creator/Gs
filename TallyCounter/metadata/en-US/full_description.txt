The most satisfying tally counter for Android — big tap button, instant haptic feedback, and everything you need to track any count in your life.

CREATE MULTIPLE COUNTERS
Name each counter with a custom emoji and label. Track reps, laps, inventory, attendance, prayer beads, bird sightings — all in one place. Switch between up to 10 counters instantly.

SET GOALS & TRACK PROGRESS
Give each counter a goal and watch the progress bar fill up. Get a coin reward when you hit your target. Every counter remembers where you left off.

FLEXIBLE STEP SIZES
Count by 1, 5, 10, 50, or 100 with one tap. Decrement to fix mistakes. Long tap-sessions record your rate per minute automatically.

SESSION HISTORY
Save any session to history with one tap. Review past sessions — name, count, and date — all saved locally on your device, no account required.

BEAUTIFUL THEMES
Unlock 8 hand-crafted colour themes using coins you earn while counting. Purple, Midnight, Crimson, Ocean, Forest, Sunset, Rose Gold, Arctic.

EARN COINS WHILE YOU COUNT
Earn coins automatically as you tap and hit goals. Use them to unlock themes or spend them in the shop. Watch a rewarded ad for a quick coin boost.

WORKS FULLY OFFLINE
No internet required. All data stays on your device. No sign-in, no sync, no fuss.
