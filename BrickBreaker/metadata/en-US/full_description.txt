The classic brick breaker game reimagined! Bounce the ball off your paddle to smash through layers of colorful bricks. Break them all to advance!

🎮 HOW TO PLAY
Drag your paddle left and right to keep the ball in play. Break all bricks to clear the level. Catch power-ups for extra balls, wider paddle, and laser shots!

✨ FEATURES
• 50+ levels with increasing challenge
• Power-ups: multi-ball, wide paddle, laser, fireball
• Combo multiplier for chain breaks
• Smooth 60fps ball physics
• Retro visual style with modern feel
• Works fully offline
