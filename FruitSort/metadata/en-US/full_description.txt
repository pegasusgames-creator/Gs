Sort colorful fruits into their matching baskets! Fruit Sort is a relaxing sorting puzzle — drag and drop fruits to organize them perfectly.

🍓 HOW TO PLAY
Each basket belongs to one type of fruit. Drag fruits from the unsorted pile and drop them in the correct basket. Sort all fruits to complete the level!

✨ FEATURES
• 150 levels with increasing complexity
• Multiple fruit types and basket sizes
• Limited moves mode for extra challenge
• Satisfying completion animations
• Hint system
• Works fully offline
