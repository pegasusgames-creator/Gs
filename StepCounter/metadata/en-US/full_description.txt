Track your daily steps with the built-in pedometer! Step Counter uses your phone's motion sensor to count steps, estimate distance, and calculate calories burned.

• Daily step goal (default 10,000 steps)
• Distance estimate (adjustable stride length)
• Calories burned estimate
• Weekly step history chart
• Daily goal streak
• Works fully offline
