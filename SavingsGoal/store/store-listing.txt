========================================
FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Reach your savings goals! Set a target amount, log every contribution, and watch your progress bar fill up toward your dream.

🐷 FEATURES
• Set a goal name, target amount, and target date
• Log deposits and track progress
• Visual progress bar
• Estimated completion date based on contribution pace
• Multiple simultaneous goals
• All data stored locally
• Works fully offline

🎯 GREAT FOR
• Emergency fund
• Vacation savings
• New phone or laptop
• Car down payment
• Wedding fund
• Any big purchase

Download free and start saving!
