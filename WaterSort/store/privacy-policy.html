Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2024

This Privacy Policy describes how Pegasus Games ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the Water Sort Puzzle mobile application (the "App"), available on Google Play under the package name com.pegasusgames.watersort.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect or store any personally identifiable information (PII).

However, the App uses third-party services that may collect data as described below.

2. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (Advertising)

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting https://www.google.com/settings/ads.

Google Play Billing

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Payment and transaction data is handled by Google. We do not receive or store your payment card information. For details, see Google Play's Terms of Service at https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/.

3. Game Data Stored Locally

All game progress (level completion, coins, settings, and daily challenge streaks) is stored locally on your device using the browser's localStorage mechanism within the App's WebView. This data is not transmitted to any server and is not accessible to us.

4. Children's Privacy

Water Sort Puzzle is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.

5. Data Retention

Local game data remains on your device until you uninstall the App or reset your progress from within the App. We do not retain any data on our servers.

6. Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect the information handled in connection with the App, including using HTTPS for all network communications and restricting the App's WebView access to local assets only.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
