Little fingers follow a glowing path, Pip the owl flaps with joy, and a new sticker lands in the book. ABC Learning turns the alphabet into a calm, playful routine for children aged 3 to 8.

TRACE EVERY LETTER
Big and little letters, A to Z, on handwriting lines with numbered start dots and arrows that show which way to go. Tracing is forgiving: a wobbly line still counts, and nothing ever says "wrong".

HEAR THE SOUNDS
Tap a letter to hear its name, tap the mouth button to hear its sound, and tap the picture to hear the word: A for apple, B for ball, all the way to Z for zebra. Every picture is original artwork drawn for this app.

PLAY THREE LETTER GAMES
- Find the letter: pop the balloon with the letter Pip asks for.
- Big and little: match a capital letter to its lowercase partner.
- First sound: look at a picture and pick the letter it starts with.
Five questions a round, with no timers, no lives and no score to lose. If a child gets stuck, the right answer gently glows.

COLLECT STICKERS
Every traced letter adds its picture sticker and every finished game adds a star sticker: 38 stickers to collect, with no coins and no shop for children to worry about.

MADE FOR SMALL HANDS
- Big buttons and pictures, so no reading is needed to find the way
- Pip the owl speaks the instructions
- Sunny, Meadow and Bedtime color themes
- Works offline

FOR GROWN-UPS
The letters, sounds and words are English (A to Z) in every language. Menus and spoken instructions follow your language when your device has a voice for it; speech uses the device's own text-to-speech voice.
Settings, purchases and links sit behind a parental gate. There are no accounts and no chat, and we collect no personal data. Ads are a child-directed Google AdMob banner that never appears during tracing or games.
Free to play: letters A to J with tracing, plus the Find the letter game. One optional purchase unlocks letters K to Z and both extra games and removes ads; a separate purchase removes ads only.
