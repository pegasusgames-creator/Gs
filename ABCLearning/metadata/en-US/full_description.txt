Learn the alphabet the fun way! ABC Learning helps young children master every letter with bright visuals, phonics sounds, and engaging quizzes.

📚 HOW IT WORKS
Swipe through all 26 letters. Each card shows the letter, a matching word, and a fun emoji. Tap the card to hear the letter sound. Progress through the alphabet at your own pace.

🔤 THREE LEARNING MODES
• Learn — Browse all 26 letters with pictures and phonics
• Quiz — Identify letters from pictures and choices
• Write — Practice tracing letters (coming soon)

✅ FEATURES
• Large, colorful letter cards
• Phonics pronunciation for every letter
• Progress bar tracks how far you've gone
• Fun emoji illustrations kids love
• Works fully offline
• No in-app timers or pressure
• Designed for ages 2–6
