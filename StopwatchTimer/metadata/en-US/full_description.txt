A precise, clean stopwatch with lap tracking and session history. Time anything — runs, workouts, cooking, sports — and keep a record of every session.

LAP TIMING
Tap Lap while the stopwatch runs to record split times. Laps are shown in a scrolling list with the current lap, your fastest (green), and slowest (red) highlighted automatically.

SESSION HISTORY
When you tap Reset, the session is automatically saved to history with the total time, number of laps, date and time. Up to 20 sessions stored.

EARN COINS
Earn coins for every session you record and every 5 laps. Use coins to unlock colour themes in the shop.

6 COLOUR THEMES
Cyan, Lime, Coral, Violet, Gold, Ice. Each changes the entire UI colour palette.

STATISTICS
Track total sessions, total laps recorded, total time measured, and coins earned.

WORKS FULLY OFFLINE
No internet required. No account needed.
