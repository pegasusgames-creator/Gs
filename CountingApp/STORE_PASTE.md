# CountingApp — store paste sheet

One file with every translated string for every locale, wrapped in
`<locale>…</locale>` blocks. Open in Play Console / App Store Connect,
find the right field, and paste the matching block for each language.

The 13 locales (Play Console tags): en-US, ar, de-DE, es-419, fr-FR, hi-IN, id, it-IT, ja-JP, pt-BR, tr-TR, uk, zh-CN

**Notes:**
- Indonesian uses tag `<id>` (not `id-ID`) — Play Console quirk.
- Ukrainian uses tag `<uk>` (not `uk-UA`) — Play Console quirk.
- Chinese uses `<zh-CN>` (Simplified). Arabic uses `<ar>` (RTL — Play
  Console renders right-to-left automatically when this locale is
  enabled).
- Title is intentionally English globally (per TRANSLATIONS.md §3) —
  not included here. Leave the title field blank for non-English
  locales; Play falls back to the default.

<en-US>
A penguin named Pebble counts out loud while your child taps each animal and object and hears every number pop. Counting Fun is a calm, playful first step into numbers for children aged 3 to 8.

WHAT CHILDREN DO
- Count: tap apples, fish, ducks and more from 1 to 20 and hear each number as it pops.
- Count to 100: add tens and ones on a hundred chart and watch the number grow.
- Sing: skip-count by 2s, 5s and 10s with short melodies that light up each number.
- Trace: follow the green dot to write the numbers 0 to 9 with a finger.
- Games: How many? Make the number. What comes next? Five questions make a round.
There are no timers, no lives and no score to lose. A wrong answer gets a gentle word and another try, and after two tries the right answer glows.

COLLECT STICKERS
44 stickers to win: number badges, traced digits, song medals and sea animals for finished rounds. There are no coins and no shop for children.

MADE FOR SMALL HANDS
- Big buttons and pictures, so no reading is needed
- Pebble speaks every instruction
- Lagoon, Sand and Night color themes
- Works offline

FOR GROWN-UPS
This app is about counting and early number sense: counting objects to 20 and numbers to 100, skip-counting by 2, 5 and 10, and writing the digits 0 to 9. It does not teach adding, subtracting or times tables. Instructions and numbers are spoken in your language when your device has a voice for it, otherwise in English, using the device's own text-to-speech. Settings, purchases and links sit behind a parental gate. There are no accounts and no chat, and the app collects no personal data. Ads are a child-directed Google AdMob banner that never appears during play.
Free to play: counting to 20, tracing 0 to 9, the 2s song and the How many? game. One optional purchase unlocks counting to 100, the 5s and 10s songs and the other two games, and removes ads; a separate purchase removes ads only.
