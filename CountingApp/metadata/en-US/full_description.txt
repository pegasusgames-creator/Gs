The simplest counting app on Android! One big button, one number. Perfect for counting anything from inventory to laps to prayers.

🔢 FEATURES
• Huge tap button — hard to miss
• Increment by 1, 5, 10, or custom amount
• Decrement/undo last tap
• Set a target count with progress indicator
• Multiple named counters
• Reset with confirmation to prevent accidents
• Count history log
• Works fully offline

✅ USES
• Inventory counting
• Lap counting
• Prayer beads / rosary
• Attendance tracking
• Reps counting at gym
• Anything you need to count!
