Fireworks is a satisfying visual experience — tap anywhere to send rockets into the night sky, then watch them explode in brilliant bursts of color. Use Auto or Ambient mode to sit back and enjoy the show, or hit Salvo for a dramatic multi-rocket launch.

BURST SHAPES
Unlock 6 explosion patterns: the classic Burst, a precise Ring, an 8-point Star, a drooping Willow, a spinning Spiral, and a Valentine Heart. Each shape changes how particles fan out and fall.

COLOR PALETTES
Six curated palettes to match your mood: Classic (vivid primaries), Sunset (warm oranges), Ocean (cool blues), Galaxy (purples and pinks), Fire (reds and ambers), and Pastel (soft tones).

FEATURES
• Tap anywhere to launch individual rockets
• Auto mode — steady stream of launches
• Ambient mode — slower, meditative pace
• Salvo — fire 5–8 rockets in quick succession
• Earn coins through use and optional rewarded ads
• Remove Ads for a cleaner screen
