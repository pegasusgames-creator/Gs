Launch dazzling fireworks with a tap! Fireworks is a beautifully animated stress-relief app — tap anywhere on the screen to send brilliant firework bursts into the night sky.

🎆 FEATURES
• Tap anywhere to launch fireworks
• 12+ firework explosion styles
• Color variety mode — rainbow explosions
• Auto mode — fireworks launch continuously
• Night sky background
• Satisfying sound effects (optional mute)
• Works fully offline

Perfect for celebrating, stress relief, or just something beautiful to look at. Download free!
