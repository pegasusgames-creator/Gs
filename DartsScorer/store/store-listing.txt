FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
The best darts scorer app for any game night! Track scores for 501, 301, and Cricket with up to 4 players. Automatic checkout suggestions included.

🎯 FEATURES
• 501 and 301 game modes
• Cricket mode (20, 19, 18, 17, 16, 15, Bull)
• Up to 4 players
• Auto-calculates remaining score
• Checkout suggestions for finishes
• Round history — undo last round
• Winner announcement
• Works fully offline

✅ GAME MODES
• 501: First to exactly zero wins. Double out required.
• 301: Shorter game, same rules.
• Cricket: Open numbers faster than your opponent.

Perfect for pub darts, home games, or practice sessions. Download free!
