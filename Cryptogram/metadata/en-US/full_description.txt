Decode famous quotes hidden in cipher text! Cryptogram gives you a substitution cipher puzzle where each letter maps to a different letter — figure out the pattern to reveal the quote.

🔐 HOW TO PLAY
Each letter in the cipher text represents a different letter of the alphabet. Tap an encrypted letter, then tap the real letter you think it maps to. Fill in all letters to reveal the hidden quote.

✨ FEATURES
• 200+ famous quotes and phrases to decode
• Categories: Inspirational, Humor, Philosophy, History, Science
• Hint system: reveal one letter at a time
• Difficulty modes: Easy (some letters pre-filled) to Hard
• Streak tracker for consecutive solves
• Works fully offline
