FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Know your exact age down to the second! Age Calculator gives you a complete breakdown of how old you are — in years, months, weeks, days, hours, and minutes.

🎂 HOW IT WORKS
Enter your date of birth and instantly see your exact age. You also get your next birthday countdown, zodiac sign, day of the week you were born, and how many days until your next birthday.

✨ FEATURES
• Exact age in years, months, weeks, days, hours & minutes
• Next birthday countdown timer
• Day of birth (what day of the week you were born)
• Zodiac sign display
• Days until next birthday
• Works fully offline
