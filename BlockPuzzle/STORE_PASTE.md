<en-US>
Three pieces. Sixty-four squares. One perfect drop.

Block Puzzle is a fast, tactile line-clearing puzzle. Drag glossy glazed blocks from your tray onto an 8×8 board. Fill a row or a column and it bursts. Fill several at once, or keep clearing move after move, and your combo multiplier climbs all the way to ×8. Wipe the whole board for a Spotless bonus.

The piece you are dragging floats just above your finger, so you always see exactly where it will land. A run only ends when nothing left in your tray fits.

A FAIR DEALER
Every tray is dealt for the board in front of you. As the board fills up, small pieces turn up more often; as your score climbs, big 3×3 squares and long bars arrive to test you. The dealer never hands you a tray where nothing fits while some piece still could, and we check that rule by simulating hundreds of seeded games before every release.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag a piece from the tray onto any open spot
• Complete a full row or column to clear it; rows and columns clear together
• Keep clearing to build a combo multiplier up to ×8
• Out of room? Revive once with a short video (it clears space and deals a fresh tray) or spend a heart

BOOSTERS
• 💣 Bomb blasts any 3×3 square (3 free to start, and the strongest spot pulses)
• ↻ Rotate turns the pieces in your tray 90°, as often as you like until the tray is used
• ⟳ Refresh swaps your tray for three new pieces dealt to fit

DAILY CHALLENGE
Every player gets the same piece sequence each day. Reach the day's target score (gentle on Monday, toughest on Sunday) to keep your streak alive, then share your result without spoilers.

MORE TO PLAY FOR
• A goal ladder on every run, with up to three stars
• 7 glaze themes: Rose Glaze, Celadon, Delft Blue, Majolica, Raku, Stoneware and Midnight Glaze, unlocked with high scores or coins
• Weekly standings, daily missions and a new themed event every month
• Crisp sound effects and haptics (switch them off in Settings)
• Plays fully offline: no Wi-Fi needed, no timer, no rush

Relaxing when you want a break, a real brain game when you chase a new record. Drop. Clear. Combo.
</en-US>

Block Puzzle 是一款节奏明快、手感十足的消行益智游戏。把托盘里亮晶晶的釉面方块拖到 8×8 棋盘上。填满一行或一列，它就会砰地消失。一次填满好几行，或者一步接一步不停消除，连击倍率最高能升到 ×8。把整个棋盘清空，还能拿到清盘奖励。

拖动中的方块悬浮在手指稍上方，落点一目了然。只有当托盘里的方块都放不下时，这一局才会结束。

公平的发牌
每个托盘都按照你眼前的棋盘来发。棋盘越满，小方块出现得越多；分数越高，大大的 3×3 方块和长条就会来考验你。只要还有方块能放下，发牌就绝不会给你一个完全放不下的托盘。每次发布前，我们都会模拟数百局游戏来检查这条规则。

玩法
• 把托盘里的方块拖到任意空位
• 填满一行或一列即可消除，行和列会同时消除
• 持续消除，把连击倍率推高到 ×8
• 没地方了？看一段短视频复活一次（腾出空间并重新发牌），或者花一颗心

道具
• 💣 炸弹：炸掉任意 3×3 区域（开局赠送 3 个，最有效的位置会闪烁提示）
• ↻ 旋转：把托盘里的方块旋转 90°，用完这一托盘前可以随便转
• ⟳ 换牌：把托盘换成三个能放下的新方块

每日挑战
每天所有玩家拿到同一组方块顺序。达到当天的目标分数（周一最轻松，周日最难）就能保住连续纪录，还能无剧透地分享成绩。

更多玩法
• 每局都有目标阶梯，最多三颗星
• 7 款釉色主题：Rose Glaze、Celadon、Delft Blue、Majolica、Raku、Stoneware 和 Midnight Glaze，可用高分或金币解锁
• 每周排名、每日任务，以及每月一场新的主题活动
• 清脆音效和振动反馈（可在设置中关闭）
• 完全离线：无需 Wi-Fi，没有计时，不用着急
