The classic block puzzle — drag wooden blocks onto the grid to complete full lines and rows. Simple to learn, impossible to put down!

🪵 HOW TO PLAY
Drag block pieces onto the 9×9 grid. Complete full horizontal or vertical lines to clear them and score points. The game ends when no more pieces can be placed.

🧩 FEATURES
• Classic 9×9 grid gameplay
• Hundreds of unique block shapes
• Score multiplier for clearing multiple lines at once
• High score tracking
• Smooth drag-and-drop controls
• Warm wood aesthetic
• Works fully offline — no wifi needed
