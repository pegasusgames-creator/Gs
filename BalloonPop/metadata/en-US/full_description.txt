Pop balloons as fast as you can! Balloon Pop is pure satisfying fun — colorful balloons float up and it's your job to tap them before they escape.

🎈 HOW TO PLAY
Tap balloons to pop them before they float off the screen. Miss too many and the game ends. The longer you last, the faster they come!

✨ FEATURES
• Colorful, animated balloons
• Multiple balloon types with different speeds
• Combo multiplier for rapid pops
• High score tracking
• Satisfying pop sound effects (optional)
• Speed increases as you progress
• Works fully offline
