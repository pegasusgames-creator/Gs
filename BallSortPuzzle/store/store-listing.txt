FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Sort colored balls into tubes until every tube holds just one color. Simple to learn — endlessly satisfying to master.

🎯 HOW TO PLAY
Tap a tube to pick up the top ball, then tap another tube to drop it. You can only place a ball on top of the same color — or into an empty tube. Sort all the tubes to complete the level.

🧩 500 LEVELS
From gentle warm-ups to brain-bending expert puzzles. Levels 1–10 ease you in with 3 colors. Later levels challenge you with 8 colors across 9 tubes. Hundreds of hours of content.

📅 DAILY CHALLENGE
A brand new puzzle every single day — the same level for everyone worldwide. Build your streak, earn bonus coins, and stay sharp. Come back tomorrow to keep it going!

🎮 DAILY MISSIONS
Three fresh missions every day: complete levels, sort balls, solve perfectly, use hints, and more. Finish them all to earn bonus coins. New missions reset every 24 hours.

🔥 DAILY LOGIN REWARDS
Collect coins just for showing up. Day 1 starts at 20 coins, growing with your streak up to 120 coins on day 7. Hit milestones at 3, 7, 14, and 30 days for massive bonus coin drops!

🛡️ STREAK SAVER
Don't want to lose your login streak? Spend a few coins to protect it — because missing one day shouldn't end a great run.

🎨 UNLOCK COLOR THEMES
Progress through levels to unlock stunning new color palettes: Neon, Pastel, Ocean, Inferno, and Galaxy. Reach level 500 to collect them all.

📊 STATS & PROGRESS TRACKING
Track your journey: levels completed, perfect 3-star clears, daily streak, total balls sorted, and more. See how far you've come and push yourself to beat your own records.

⚡ HOW DO YOU COMPARE?
After each level, see your move count vs. par — and find out if you're in the Top 10%, Top 25%, or Top 50% of solvers. Optimize your strategy and chase that perfect clear.
