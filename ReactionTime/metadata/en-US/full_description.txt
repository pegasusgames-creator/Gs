Reaction Time measures how quickly you can tap when the screen turns green. Sessions run 3, 5 or 10 trials. Each trial randomizes the wait time so you can't predict the signal — tapping too early counts as a false start.

HOW TO PLAY
Tap the screen to start. Wait for it to turn green, then tap as fast as you can. Your reaction time in milliseconds appears instantly. After all trials, you get a rating and session average.

FEATURES
• 3, 5 or 10 trial modes
• Randomized 1–5 second wait to prevent guessing
• False start detection (purple screen)
• Millisecond precision using performance.now()
• Session history with date, average, and rating
• All-time record tracking
• 6 colour themes for the play screen

RATINGS
Lightning (under 180ms), Elite (under 220ms), Fast (under 280ms), Average (under 350ms), Slow, Keep Going

COIN REWARDS
Earn coins each session. Faster averages earn more coins. Use coins to unlock colour themes.
