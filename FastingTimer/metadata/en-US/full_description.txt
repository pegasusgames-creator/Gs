The clean, simple fasting tracker for intermittent fasting! Start your fast, watch the timer count up, and know exactly when your eating window opens.

⏱️ FASTING PROTOCOLS
• 16:8 — fast 16 hours, eat in 8-hour window
• 18:6 — fast 18 hours, eat in 6-hour window
• 20:4 — extended fast
• OMAD — one meal a day (23:1)
• Custom — set your own fasting duration

✨ FEATURES
• Visual timer showing fast progress
• Eating window display
• Start/stop with time saved
• Fasting history log
• Streak counter
• Notification when fast is complete
• Works fully offline
