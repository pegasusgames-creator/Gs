One tap a day is all it takes. Habit Tracker turns the things you want to do — drink more water, read before bed, move every morning — into a calm daily checklist with streaks that keep you coming back.

HOW IT WORKS
• Create a habit in seconds: name, icon, color, the days it repeats and a daily goal — once, or a count like 8 glasses.
• Tap the circle to check in. Count goals fill up as you go, and finishing one gives a satisfying pop and a light vibration.
• Long-press to log an exact count or add a note about how it went.
• Forgot yesterday? Fix any past day from the calendar.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Current and longest streak for every habit. Days a habit isn't scheduled never break a streak.
• A monthly calendar heatmap per habit.
• 30-day completion rate, total check-ins and perfect days.
• A 12-week trend per habit and a 14-day overview of all of them.

FITS YOUR LIFE
• Repeat every day, on weekdays, on weekends or on the days you pick.
• Optional reminders per habit at the time you choose, plus an optional daily check-in reminder. Nothing nags you unless you switch it on.
• Archive paused habits and keep their history; drag to reorder.
• Light and dark themes, accent colors, adjustable text size, 13 languages.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• No account, no sign-in. Everything is stored on your phone and works offline.
• Back up to text you can share or copy, and restore it on any device.
