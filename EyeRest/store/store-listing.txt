Eye Rest — 20-20-20 Rule Timer

========================================
SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars max)
========================================
Protect your eyes! Reminds you to take screen breaks.

========================================
FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Protect your eyes from screen fatigue! Eye Rest reminds you to follow the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds.

👁️ FEATURES
• 20-20-20 rule timer with gentle notification
• Customizable interval (10–60 minutes)
• Guided eye exercise animations
• Daily screen time tracker
• Reminder streak to build the habit
• Works in background
• Works fully offline

👁️ EYE EXERCISES INCLUDED
• Focus shifting (near to far)
• Eye rolling
• Palming
• Blinking exercises
