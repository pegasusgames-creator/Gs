FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
The fastest aspect ratio calculator on your phone! Enter any two dimensions and instantly get the ratio, the missing dimension, or the scaled size.

📐 FEATURES
• Calculate aspect ratio from width × height
• Scale dimensions while keeping ratio locked
• Common presets: 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 9:16, 21:9
• Find missing dimension from ratio + one side
• Copy results to clipboard
• Works fully offline

Perfect for designers, video editors, photographers, and developers who need quick ratio calculations on the go.
