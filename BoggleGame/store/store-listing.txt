FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
The classic Boggle word game on your phone! Swipe through connected letters to form words and score points before the timer runs out.

🔠 HOW TO PLAY
Letters are arranged in a 4×4 grid. Trace a path through adjacent letters (including diagonals) to spell valid words. Longer words score more points!

✨ FEATURES
• 4×4 letter grid with randomized layouts
• Validated word list — only real words count
• Score multipliers for long words
• Timer with adjustable duration
• High score tracking
• Hint to reveal one valid word
• Works fully offline

Sharpen your vocabulary and beat your high score. Download free!
