FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Roar! Explore the world of dinosaurs! Kids can learn about 50+ prehistoric creatures with fun facts, amazing images, and dinosaur sounds.

🦕 FEATURES
• 50+ dinosaur species with detailed fact cards
• Tap to hear each dinosaur's roar/sound
• Quiz mode: identify dinosaurs from clues
• Categories: Carnivores, Herbivores, Flying, Sea creatures
• Time period display (Triassic, Jurassic, Cretaceous)
• Fun size comparison to everyday objects
• Works fully offline

🦖 DINOSAURS INCLUDE
T-Rex, Triceratops, Stegosaurus, Brachiosaurus, Velociraptor, Pterodactyl, Ankylosaurus, Spinosaurus, and many more!

Perfect for curious kids ages 4–12. Download free!
