Who was longer than a school bus? Tap a Tyrannosaurus and find out. Dinosaur World is a calm, picture-first explorer for children aged 3 to 8, with 34 dinosaurs and 5 other ancient animals drawn as original illustrations, and a baby dino named Tiko who says hello and suggests who to meet next.

MEET THE DINOSAURS
Open a dinosaur's card for a big picture, how long it was, how much it weighed, what it ate, which time period it lived in and where its fossils were found. Tiko reads the facts aloud. Every fact is checked against palaeontology references. Not every ancient animal was a dinosaur, so Pteranodon and Quetzalcoatlus are labelled flying reptiles, Elasmosaurus and Mosasaurus sea reptiles, and Dimetrodon an animal that lived before the dinosaurs.

HOW BIG?
Slide from a tiny Compsognathus to a giant Argentinosaurus and see each one next to a school bus, a child or a house, drawn at the same scale.

DIG FOR FOSSILS
Brush the sand away with a finger to uncover a buried fossil.

MATCH THE SHADOWS
Match each dinosaur to its shadow. No timers, no lives and no score to lose: a wrong tap just tries again.

COLLECT STICKERS
Every fossil and every match adds a sticker: 47 to collect, with no coins and no shop for children.

MADE FOR SMALL HANDS
- Big buttons and pictures, so no reading is needed
- Tiko speaks the names and facts
- Sand, Fern and Volcano color themes
- Works offline

FOR GROWN-UPS
Nobody knows how dinosaurs really sounded. The roars are made up and made by the app; they are not recordings. Menus and spoken facts follow your language (13 languages) when your device has a voice for it, using the device's own text-to-speech voice. Dinosaur names stay as scientific names.
Settings, purchases and links sit behind a parental gate. There are no accounts and no chat, and we collect no personal data. Ads are a child-directed Google AdMob banner that never appears during the games.
Free to play: 16 dinosaurs plus Pteranodon, the size slider and both games. One optional purchase unlocks all 39 animals and removes ads; a separate purchase removes ads only.
