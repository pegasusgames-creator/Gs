FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
Express your creativity with dots! Dot Art is a fun, relaxing pixel drawing app where you place colored dots to create pixel art masterpieces.

🎨 FEATURES
• Multiple canvas sizes (16×16, 32×32, 64×64)
• Full color palette with 64 colors
• Erase, fill bucket, and eyedropper tools
• Undo/redo history
• Grid display toggle
• Save artwork to gallery
• Share creations
• Works fully offline

Perfect for beginner pixel artists, kids, and anyone who loves creative apps. Download free!
