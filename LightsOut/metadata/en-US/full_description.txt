The classic Lights Out puzzle. Tap a light to toggle it — but every tap also flips the lights directly above, below, left, and right. Turn them all off to solve the level.

HOW TO PLAY
Tap any cell to toggle it and its four neighbours. Work out the right sequence of taps to turn every light off. The fewer moves you use, the more coins you earn.

100 SOLVABLE LEVELS
All 100 levels are guaranteed solvable. Difficulty ramps gradually from easy 5-tap configurations to complex 17-tap arrangements.

HINT SYSTEM
Stuck? Tap Hint to highlight the single cell that reduces the most lit lights. No hints used on a level earns a clean solve bonus.

6 LIGHT THEMES
Classic Yellow, Neon Blue, Matrix Green, Alarm Red, Plasma Purple, Arctic White. Unlock themes with coins you earn while solving puzzles.

STATISTICS
Track levels solved, total moves made, hints used, and coins earned.

WORKS FULLY OFFLINE
No internet required. No account needed. Pure puzzle solving.
