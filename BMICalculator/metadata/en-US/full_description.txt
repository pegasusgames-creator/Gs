Type your height and weight and see your BMI at once, with the WHO adult category it falls in and a gauge that shows where you sit. Switch between metric and imperial any time. Everything is calculated on your phone, works offline and needs no account.

WHAT YOU GET
• Live result as you type, with a colour gauge and the 18.5, 25 and 30 lines marked
• Category named in neutral WHO wording, for example "this value is categorised as Normal range"
• The weight range that gives a BMI of 18.5 to 24.9 at your height, and how far your weight is from it
• History: save a reading with a note and follow your BMI or weight on a chart
• Goal: set a target weight and follow your progress
• Optional body-fat estimate using the Deurenberg formula (1.20 × BMI + 0.23 × age − 10.8 × sex − 5.4), always labelled "estimate"
• Backup and restore, light and dark themes, 13 languages, optional weekly reminder to log a reading

PLEASE NOTE
BMI is a screening number, not a diagnosis. It is not valid for children, during pregnancy or for athletes, and it says nothing on its own about body fat, muscle or health. The body-fat figure is a rough statistical estimate, not a measurement. This app gives no medical advice. Talk to a health professional about your health.
