========================================
FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
The party game everyone loves! Hold your phone to your forehead, and get your team to describe the word so you can guess it before the timer runs out!

🙆 HOW TO PLAY
Tilt phone down to your forehead — timer starts! Tilt up if you pass. Guess as many as possible in 60 seconds. Keep score as you go!
