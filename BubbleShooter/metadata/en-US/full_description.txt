One clean bank shot off the wall, and half the board comes crashing down. That moment is what Bubble Shooter is built around.

Drag to aim. A dotted line shows your path — including the bounce off the side wall — and a ghost bubble marks exactly where your shot will stick. Let go, land next to two or more of the same colour, and they pop. Anything left hanging with nothing to hold it falls with them, worth double points. Pop on consecutive shots to build a combo up to ×3.

THREE KINDS OF GOAL
• Clear the board — pop and drop every bubble.
• Free the Pips — tiny round critters are trapped in glass bubbles near the top. Pop next to them, or knock them loose, to set them free.
• Reach the score — hit the target before your bubbles run out (clearing the board wins too).

500 LEVELS
Six colours, eleven board shapes — hearts, arches, chandeliers, pillars, lattices and more — and a steady climb from three colours to six. Every level is played by our test bot before it ships and cleared within its bubble limit, so no board is a dead end. Use fewer bubbles to earn up to three stars.

BOOSTERS
• Colour swap — always free: tap the launcher to swap with the next bubble.
• Fireball — burns straight through every bubble in its path, bouncing off the walls.
• Bomb — explodes where it lands and clears two rings of bubbles.
• Rainbow — matches any colour and pops every group it touches.

MORE WAYS TO PLAY
• Daily board — the same board for everyone each day, gentle on Monday and tough by Sunday. Keep a streak for bonus coins.
• Endless — survival mode: a new row slides in every five misses. How long can you last?

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
• Satisfying synthesized pops that climb in pitch as a cluster bursts
• Optional vibration, and colour symbols on every bubble for colour-blind players
• Seven board themes, including Midnight
• Plays offline — no account, no sign-in
