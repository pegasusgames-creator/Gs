FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
The most satisfying bubble shooter on Android! Aim and shoot colored bubbles to match three or more of the same color. Pop them all to clear the level!

🫧 HOW TO PLAY
Drag your finger to aim the shooter at the bubble cluster. Match 3+ bubbles of the same color to pop them. Clear the board before bubbles reach the bottom line.

✨ FEATURES
• 200+ carefully designed levels
• Smooth aiming with trajectory guide
• Special bubbles: bombs, rainbows, stone
• Chain reactions for huge combos
• Daily bonus level
• Works fully offline
