Don't Tap White — Piano Tiles

========================================
SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars max)
========================================

========================================
FULL DESCRIPTION
========================================
The addictive piano tiles game — tap the black tiles as fast as possible without touching the white ones! One mistake and it's game over.

⬛ HOW TO PLAY
Tiles scroll down the screen. Tap every black tile. Never tap white. Go as fast as you can — your speed is the score!

✨ FEATURES
• Multiple modes: Classic, Arcade, Rush
• Speed progressively increases
• High score tracking
• Combo multiplier for consecutive perfect taps
• Reaction time statistics
• Works fully offline

⚡ GAME MODES
• Classic: tap as fast as possible, one miss = game over
• Arcade: tap to the beat of the music
• Rush: limited time, maximum tiles
