The addictive piano tiles reflex game — tap every black tile as it scrolls down. Miss one or touch white, and it's over. Simple rule, endless challenge.

⬛ HOW TO PLAY
Black tiles scroll toward you. Tap every black tile before it passes. Never tap white. The faster you tap, the higher you score.

4 GAME MODES
• Classic: speed increases with every tile — how far can you go?
• Arcade: starts blazing fast for maximum reaction-time challenge
• Lives: 3 lives — mistakes cost a life, not the run
• Zen: slow and relaxing — no game over, just flow

6 COLOUR THEMES
Classic, Mono, Neon, Fire, Ocean, and Pastel. Unlock themes with coins you earn by playing.

EARN COINS WHILE YOU PLAY
Earn coins every 10 tiles and through consecutive good runs. Use them to unlock the premium colour themes.

PARTICLE EFFECTS
Each successful tap spawns a satisfying burst of colour matching your current theme.

HIGH SCORE TRACKING
High score tracked per game mode. Improve your score to earn bonus coins.

WORKS FULLY OFFLINE
No account required. All progress saved locally on your device.
