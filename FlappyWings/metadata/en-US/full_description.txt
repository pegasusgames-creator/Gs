One tap. One paper bird. A sky full of floating cloud islands.

Flappy Wings is a calm-looking, quick-thinking arcade flyer. Tap to lift your folded paper bird, let go to glide, and slip through the gap in every cloud-island gate. Each gate is a point. How far can you fly?

TAP, GLIDE, THREAD THE GATES
• One-touch control that is easy to learn and hard to put down
• Wide, friendly gaps at the start, then a smooth, gradual climb in challenge
• Later gates drift up and down, so timing matters as much as rhythm
• Smooth 60 Hz flight physics that feel the same on every phone

FEATHERS, MEDALS AND SKINS
• Catch feathers mid-flight: each one is a coin
• Earn bronze at 10 gates, silver at 25, gold at 50 and platinum at 100
• Spend coins on seven sky skins, from Apricot Dawn to Midnight and Aurora. Each one recolours the sky, the islands and your bird

A DAILY FLIGHT FOR EVERYONE
Every day brings one fixed course with a target score, the same for every player. Hit the target to grow your streak and earn bonus coins. Targets rise from Monday to Sunday.

SECOND CHANCES, YOUR WAY
• Arm a shield before take-off to shrug off one hit
• One free revive per flight with an optional short video
• Hearts let you continue a crashed flight, up to three times
• Starting a new flight is always free

MORE TO DO
• Daily missions, monthly events and weekly standings
• Vibration and sound you can switch off in Settings
• Plays fully offline, no account needed

Fold. Flap. Float through the sunset.
